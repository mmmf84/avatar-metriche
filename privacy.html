Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

1. Who is responsible

Avatar Metrics is operated by Franco Meli, an individual based in Italy (contact: francomeli@hotmail.it).

2. What data is collected

Only from accounts owned by the operator that have authorized the app:

  • basic profile information (account ID, username, display name);
  • the list of published videos and their statistics: views, likes, comments, shares, duration, publication date and link.

The app does not collect data about other users, followers or commenters, and does not read private messages.

3. Why

The data is used only to measure how the operator's own videos perform and to plan future content.

4. Where it is kept

Data and access tokens are stored locally on the operator's personal computer. They are not uploaded to third-party servers, sold, shared or used for advertising.

5. How long

Statistics are kept as long as they are useful for this analysis. Access tokens are deleted when access is revoked.

6. Your choices

Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok or Instagram account settings. To request deletion of any stored data, write to francomeli@hotmail.it.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
