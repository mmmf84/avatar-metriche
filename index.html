Avatar Metrics

Avatar Metrics is a personal, non-commercial tool run by Franco Meli. It reads the public performance statistics (views, likes, comments, shares) of the TikTok and Instagram accounts that Franco owns and manages, so he can see which of his videos work best.

It is not offered to the public: the only accounts that authorize it are Franco's own.
